Figure 1 | Experimental timing and example behavioural responses.

(A) Preparation and illumination schematic. CS, conditioned stimulus; US, unconditioned stimulus. Basal illumination is indicated separately. (B) Training-trial timing for unpaired control, Delay, 3sTrace and 10sTrace conditions. The CS lasts 10 s; US onset is 9, 13 or 20 s relative to CS onset in the three paired conditions, respectively. Control CS timing varies relative to the long-US schedule. The pale control dots illustrate alternative US onsets in separate trials, not three pulses in a single trial; question marks indicate unknown latency. (C) Session sequence, shown schematically rather than to scale. Priming/habituation includes four CS-like presentations, including left-side illumination, and 15 separate short US pulses. Pre-training includes 10 CS presentations and three separate short US pulses; training includes 50 CS presentations, 46 paired long US pulses and four CS-only catch trials; testing includes 30 CS presentations and 14 separate short US pulses. Short and long US pulses last 50 and 100 ms, respectively. Interspersed short US pulses are not conditioning pairings. In controls, long-US times follow the paired schedule while training CS onsets are randomized per fish.

(D) Distal cumulative tail-angle traces from an example Delay fish (20221115_07), centred on each trial’s median over [−15, 0) s before CS onset. (E) Scaled-vigor black traces for the same five displayed trials (5, 13, 59, 62 and 89; acquisition trials 9, 17, 63, 66 and 93), paired with the corresponding exact rows from G as colour bands. Black traces use D’s stored vigor and measured CS-relative frame times. The inherited detected-bout eligibility mask is joined by acquisition trial and frame ID; unmatched, ineligible, nonfinite and nonpositive values are excluded. For each trial, B is the median eligible raw vigor across baseline frames in [−15, 0) s. Each eligible raw frame is centred as V−B, then scaled by 0.7 / max(−P10, P90), where the linear quantiles use the unbinned baseline-frame raw deviations. No logarithm is applied to the black trace. No temporal binning, smoothing or resampling enters the black trace, its baseline or its scaling. A nonempty eligible baseline and noncollapsed raw-deviation spread are required; baseline frame counts and time coverage are recorded in the scoped E freeze evidence. The shared 0 to 5 display axis places raw vigor equal to B at zero at the bottom. The upper plot boundary visually clips 230 high-valued frames; 28789 frames fall below the lower boundary. All numerical values remain unchanged. The dashed baseline reference is omitted. The color legend is copied from the shared F–H colourbar and applies only to the G-matched color bands. Each CS boundary is drawn once continuously across all traces and bands. Each 40-cell colour band copies the frozen Delay source’s 1 s row values and exact rendered cell RGB, retaining the Delay source’s accepted cadence-reconstructed timing and baseline-bin median subtraction. Thus the band uses a separate normalization from the black trace. Band colours saturate at ±0.25 log units; black cells indicate missing bins. CS onset and offset are green guides (offset dashed), and actual US onset is purple, using D’s measured event times. Panel E is frozen in this scoped selection; earlier E sources and F–H remain preserved.

(F–H) Frozen example heatmaps for Control (F; 20221115_09), Delay (G; 20221115_07) and 3sTrace (H; 20230307_12). Finite, strictly positive, valid adjacent detected-bout frames of legacy distal angular vigor are natural-log transformed. Each left-inclusive, right-exclusive 1 s bin is the median of eligible original log frames. Subtract the median of finite baseline-bin medians over [−15, 0) s, with one vote per finite bin. One finite baseline bin is sufficient; trials without baseline data are undefined. No amplitude scaling or numerical clipping is applied. Native managua_r has zero at the midpoint of the −0.25 to +0.25 log-unit range; colors saturate beyond those limits. Black cells denote missing data, not zero activity. Established cadence-reconstructed timing and the movement mask are retained. Green lines show nominal CS onset and 10 s offset (dashed); violet dotted guides show expected US during training. Arrowheads in G mark the five Delay trials shown in D/E. Horizontal boundaries separate Pre, Train and Test. These descriptive examples do not support population-level inference. The scoped F–H and E freezes do not freeze the whole figure.

Displayed trial numbers in D–H exclude acquisition trials 1–4 (displayed trial = acquisition trial − 4). F–H therefore run from 1 to 90, and the D/E examples and marked G rows are 5, 13, 59, 62 and 89. Original acquisition IDs 9, 17, 63, 66 and 93 remain in the source mappings. In B, t (s) is relative to CS onset, with t = 0 at CS onset.

Figure 2 | Population-level conditioning measures and exploratory statistical comparisons.

(A) Delay and matched control population heatmaps (29 Delay fish; 28 controls). Each fish/trial uses the selected Figure 1 F–H pipeline: cadence-reconstructed timing, legacy distal angular vigor in rad/ms and finite positive valid adjacent detected-bout frames; natural log per original frame; direct medians within left-inclusive/right-exclusive 1 s bins; subtract the median of finite baseline-bin medians over [−15, 0) s, each finite bin one vote. One baseline bin is sufficient; an empty baseline makes the whole fish/trial undefined. Each population cell is the arithmetic mean of available fish-level centred values, with one vote per contributing fish. Missing cells are black; contributing-fish counts are retained in the associated data. No amplitude scaling or numerical clipping; native managua_r saturates visually at −0.25 and +0.25. Display trials 1–90 map to acquisition trials 5–94. Green guides show CS onset and nominal 10 s offset; the dotted violet guide marks the expected 9 s US during paired Delay training. White boundaries separate Pre, Train and Test. Panel A is frozen. The six-heatmap row uses a single shared managua_r colourbar at the far right (−0.25 to +0.25 natural-log vigor difference); A and B retain their distinct estimators.

(B) 3sTrace (40 fish) versus its matched control cohort (19 fish) retains the earlier signed bout-log-vigor calculation in 0.5 s bins, referenced to [−15, 0) s; population values average fish equally. Its earlier data are retained at the author’s request. B and C will later adopt A’s individual-fish pipeline and pooled estimator. B is unfrozen. (C) 10sTrace population heatmaps: pending authenticated inputs; no result is shown.

(D, E) Block comparisons for Delay and 3sTrace, respectively, with Control on the left and the experimental condition on the right. For each eligible trial, the outcome is the median natural-log vigor among positive valid response-bout frames minus the corresponding median over baseline-bout frames. Baseline is [−15, 0) s; response windows are [0, 9) s for Delay and [0, 13) s for 3sTrace. Non-bout, invalid and zero samples are excluded before logging; an empty window is undefined. Fish-level values are medians across eligible trials in Pre-training (PT, trials 10–14), Early Test (ET, 65–69) and Late Test (LT, 90–94), requiring at least three eligible trials per Delay block and one per 3sTrace block. Thin trajectories connect fish; black summaries show fish medians and interquartile ranges. Contributing fish counts in PT/ET/LT order are: D, Delay 29/29/29 and control 28/24/26; E, 3sTrace 40/40/40 and control 19/19/18. Zero denotes no response-minus-baseline difference.

D/E statistical annotations represent 36 two-sided comparisons in one Holm family. Exact sign tests assess the zero reference and paired changes; Brunner–Munzel t tests assess independent block values and changes. A dagger identifies a comparison with zero. Stars on comparison lines identify the linked contrast: * adjusted p < 0.05; ** < 0.01; *** < 0.001; **** < 0.0001. These analyses assume fish independence, do not adjust for day/tank and do not establish equivalence. (F) 10sTrace block comparison: pending authenticated inputs; no result is shown. When populated, Control precedes 10sTrace.

(G) Historical LogMedian analysis for the Delay experiment (28 control fish; 29 Delay fish). Each fish/trial outcome is median ln positive response-bout vigor minus median ln positive baseline-bout vigor, followed by the median across fish; windows are [0, 9) and [−15, 0) s. Curves retain missing trials, without smoothing or a second log transform. Shaded intervals are pointwise percentile 95% confidence intervals from 5,000 whole-fish trajectory resamples within condition (seed 10). Of 5,130 scheduled fish/trial observations, 4,811 are eligible. Pre denotes pre-training; Tr1–Tr5 and Te1–Te3 denote successive training and test blocks.

The statistical rows in G retain distinct hypotheses: D, baseline-adjusted condition-by-block interaction relative to Pre with fish random intercept and slope (Holm correction across eight tests); M, local condition difference at centred trial with a fish random intercept (Benjamini–Hochberg correction across nine tests); R, local condition slope difference, shown raw and corrected across nine tests. The trial-change row uses a phase-aware, baseline-adjusted mixed-effects model with fish random intercept and slope, testing control-minus-Delay change relative to average Pre trials 5–14 (Benjamini–Hochberg across 90 trials). In G, *, ** and *** denote p < 0.05, 0.01 and 0.001 within the indicated family. “n/a” marks unavailable local M/R results in Test3 because of singular fits. Heavy residual tails and exploratory model status limit interpretation; significance marks do not establish learning onset.

(H) 3sTrace response/baseline trajectory (19 control fish; 40 3sTrace fish): response total activity over [0, 13) s divided by baseline total activity over [−15, 0) s, computed per fish/trial and summarized by the condition median with fish interquartile range. This ratio is a different outcome from D/E and G, not a replication of their log-median result. (I) 10sTrace trajectory: pending authenticated inputs; no result is shown.
