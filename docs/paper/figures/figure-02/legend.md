# Figure 2 legend draft

Figure 2 | Population-level conditioning measures and exploratory statistical comparisons.

(A) Delay and matched control population heatmaps (29 Delay fish; 28 controls). Each fish/trial uses the selected Figure 1 F–H pipeline: cadence-reconstructed timing, legacy distal angular vigor in rad/ms and finite positive valid adjacent detected-bout frames; natural log per original frame; direct medians within left-inclusive/right-exclusive 1 s bins; subtract the median of finite baseline-bin medians over [−15, 0) s, each finite bin one vote. One baseline bin is sufficient; an empty baseline makes the whole fish/trial undefined. Each population cell is the arithmetic mean of available fish-level centred values, with one vote per contributing fish. Missing cells are black; contributing-fish counts are retained in the associated data. No amplitude scaling or numerical clipping; native managua_r saturates visually at −0.25 and +0.25. Display trials 1–90 map to acquisition trials 5–94. Green guides show CS onset and nominal 10 s offset; the dotted violet guide marks the expected 9 s US during paired Delay training. White boundaries separate Pre, Train and Test. Panel A is frozen. The six-heatmap row uses a single shared managua_r colourbar at the far right (−0.25 to +0.25 natural-log vigor difference); A and B retain their distinct estimators.

(B) 3 s Trace (40 fish) versus its matched control cohort (19 fish) retains the earlier signed bout-log-vigor calculation in 0.5 s bins, referenced to [−15, 0) s; population values average fish equally. Its earlier data are retained at the author’s request. B and C will later adopt A’s individual-fish pipeline and pooled estimator. B is unfrozen. (C) 10 s Trace population heatmaps: pending authenticated inputs; no result is shown.

(D, E) Block comparisons for Delay and 3 s Trace, respectively, with Control on the left and the experimental condition on the right. For each eligible trial, the outcome is the median natural-log vigor among positive valid response-bout frames minus the corresponding median over baseline-bout frames. Baseline is [−15, 0) s; response windows are [0, 9) s for Delay and [0, 13) s for 3 s Trace. Non-bout, invalid and zero samples are excluded before logging; an empty window is undefined. Fish-level values are medians across eligible trials in Pre-training (PT, trials 10–14), Early Test (ET, 65–69) and Late Test (LT, 90–94), requiring at least three eligible trials per Delay block and one per 3 s Trace block. Thin trajectories connect fish; black summaries show fish medians and interquartile ranges. Contributing fish counts in PT/ET/LT order are: D, Delay 29/29/29 and control 28/24/26; E, 3 s Trace 40/40/40 and control 19/19/18. Zero denotes no response-minus-baseline difference.

D/E statistical annotations represent 36 two-sided comparisons in one Holm family. Exact sign tests assess the zero reference and paired changes; Brunner–Munzel t tests assess independent block values and changes. A dagger identifies a comparison with zero. Stars on comparison lines identify the linked contrast: * adjusted p < 0.05; ** < 0.01; *** < 0.001; **** < 0.0001. These analyses assume fish independence, do not adjust for day/tank and do not establish equivalence. (F) 10 s Trace block comparison: pending authenticated inputs; no result is shown. When populated, Control precedes 10 s Trace.

(G) Historical LogMedian analysis for Delay and its control cohort. Each fish/trial outcome is median ln positive response-bout vigor minus median ln positive baseline-bout vigor, followed by the median across fish; windows are [0, 9) and [−15, 0) s. Curves retain missing trials, without smoothing or a second log transform. Shaded intervals are pointwise percentile 95% confidence intervals from 5,000 whole-fish trajectory resamples within condition (seed 10). Of 5,130 scheduled fish/trial observations, 4,811 are eligible. Pre denotes pre-training; Tr1–Tr5 and Te1–Te3 denote successive training and test blocks.

The statistical rows in G retain distinct hypotheses: D, baseline-adjusted condition-by-block interaction relative to Pre with fish random intercept and slope (Holm correction across eight tests); M, local condition difference at centred trial with a fish random intercept (Benjamini–Hochberg correction across nine tests); R, local condition slope difference, shown raw and corrected across nine tests. The trial-change row uses a phase-aware, baseline-adjusted mixed-effects model with fish random intercept and slope, testing control-minus-Delay change relative to average Pre trials 5–14 (Benjamini–Hochberg across 90 trials). In G, *, ** and *** denote p < 0.05, 0.01 and 0.001 within the indicated family. “n/a” marks unavailable local M/R results in Test3 because of singular fits. Heavy residual tails and exploratory model status limit interpretation; significance marks do not establish learning onset.

(H) 3 s Trace response/baseline trajectory: response total activity over [0, 13) s divided by baseline total activity over [−15, 0) s, computed per fish/trial and summarized by the condition median with fish interquartile range. This ratio is a different outcome from D/E and G, not a replication of their log-median result. (I) 10 s Trace trajectory: pending authenticated inputs; no result is shown.
