Figure 2 | Population conditioning measures and exploratory comparisons.

(a) Delay (29 fish) and matched control (28 fish) heatmaps: equal-fish means of baseline-centred, direct 1-s median log vigor. (b) 3sTrace (40 fish) and control (19 fish) retain the earlier signed bout-log-vigor estimator in 0.5-s bins; this unfrozen estimator differs from a. (c, f, i) 10sTrace inputs are pending; no results are shown. Heatmap colours saturate at ±0.25; black denotes missing data. Green/violet guides indicate CS/expected US; boundaries separate session phases.

(d, e) Delay and 3sTrace block comparisons, with Control first. Outcomes are median ln positive response-bout vigor minus baseline ln vigor: baseline [−15, 0) s; responses [0, 9) or [0, 13) s. PT/ET/LT denote pre-training/early/late test. Thin lines connect fish; black summaries show medians and interquartile ranges. Fish counts PT/ET/LT: d, Control 28/24/26, Delay 29/29/29; e, Control 19/19/18, 3sTrace 40/40/40. Dagger: comparison against zero. Stars: Holm-adjusted significance across 36 comparisons (*p<0.05, **p<0.01, ***p<0.001, ****p<0.0001).

(g) Delay experiment: medians of response-minus-baseline ln vigor across 28 control/29 Delay fish; shaded pointwise 95% bootstrap confidence intervals (5,000 whole-fish resamples). Statistical rows distinguish block interaction (D), block mean (M), slope (R) and trial change; correction families differ. Here *, ** and *** indicate p<0.05, 0.01 and 0.001; n/a denotes singular fits. Exploratory significance does not establish learning onset.

(h) 3sTrace/control median response-to-baseline total-activity ratios with fish interquartile ranges; this differs from the log differences in d/e/g. Full eligibility, block windows, statistical hypotheses and correction families remain in the figure-specific Methods record.
