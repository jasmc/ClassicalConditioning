# Figure 2 legend draft

Figure 2 | Population-level conditioning measures and exploratory statistical comparisons.

(A, B) CS-aligned population heatmaps for Delay (29 fish) versus its matched unpaired control cohort (28 fish), and 3 s Trace (40 fish) versus its matched control cohort (19 fish). Each fish contributes signed bout-log-vigor values in 0.5 s bins, referenced to [−15, 0) s before CS onset; displayed population values average fish equally. These heatmap definitions use no P10/P90 scaling and differ from Figure 1’s example heatmaps. Black cells indicate missing values. (C) 10 s Trace population heatmaps: pending authenticated inputs; no result is shown.

(D, E) Block comparisons for Delay and 3 s Trace. For each eligible trial, the outcome is the median natural-log vigor among positive valid response-bout frames minus the corresponding median over baseline-bout frames. Baseline is [−15, 0) s; response windows are [0, 9) s for Delay and [0, 13) s for 3 s Trace. Non-bout, invalid and zero samples are excluded before logging; an empty window is undefined. Fish-level values are medians across eligible trials in Pre-training (PT, trials 10–14), Early Test (ET, 65–69) and Late Test (LT, 90–94), requiring at least three eligible trials per Delay block and one per 3 s Trace block. Thin trajectories connect fish; black summaries show fish medians and interquartile ranges. Displayed block counts identify contributing fish. Zero denotes no response-minus-baseline difference.

D/E statistical annotations represent 36 two-sided comparisons in one Holm family. Exact sign tests assess the zero reference and paired changes; Brunner–Munzel t tests assess independent block values and changes. A dagger identifies a comparison with zero. Stars on comparison lines identify the linked contrast: * adjusted p < 0.05; ** < 0.01; *** < 0.001; **** < 0.0001. These analyses assume fish independence, do not adjust for day/tank and do not establish equivalence. (F) 10 s Trace block comparison: pending authenticated inputs; no result is shown.

(G) Historical LogMedian analysis for Delay and its control cohort. Each fish/trial outcome is median ln positive response-bout vigor minus median ln positive baseline-bout vigor, followed by the median across fish; windows are [0, 9) and [−15, 0) s. Curves retain missing trials, without smoothing or a second log transform. Shaded intervals are pointwise percentile 95% confidence intervals from 5,000 whole-fish trajectory resamples within condition (seed 10). Of 5,130 scheduled fish/trial observations, 4,811 are eligible. Pre denotes pre-training; Tr1–Tr5 and Te1–Te3 denote successive training and test blocks.

The statistical rows in G retain distinct hypotheses: D, baseline-adjusted condition-by-block interaction relative to Pre with fish random intercept and slope (Holm correction across eight tests); M, local condition difference at centred trial with a fish random intercept (Benjamini–Hochberg correction across nine tests); R, local condition slope difference, shown raw and corrected across nine tests. The trial-change row uses a phase-aware, baseline-adjusted mixed-effects model with fish random intercept and slope, testing control-minus-Delay change relative to average Pre trials 5–14 (Benjamini–Hochberg across 90 trials). In G, *, ** and *** denote p < 0.05, 0.01 and 0.001 within the indicated family. “n/a” marks unavailable local M/R results in Test3 because of singular fits. Heavy residual tails and exploratory model status limit interpretation; significance marks do not establish learning onset.

(H) 3 s Trace response/baseline trajectory: response total activity over [0, 13) s divided by baseline total activity over [−15, 0) s, computed per fish/trial and summarized by the condition median with fish interquartile range. This ratio is a different outcome from D/E and G, not a replication of their log-median result. (I) 10 s Trace trajectory: pending authenticated inputs; no result is shown.
