# Figure 1 legend draft

Figure 1 | Experimental timing and example behavioural responses.

(A) Preparation and illumination schematic. CS, conditioned stimulus; US, unconditioned stimulus. Basal illumination is indicated separately. (B) Training-trial timing for unpaired control, Delay, 3 s Trace and 10 s Trace conditions. The CS lasts 10 s; US onset is 9, 13 or 20 s relative to CS onset in the three paired conditions, respectively. Control CS timing varies relative to the long-US schedule. The pale control dots illustrate alternative US onsets in separate trials, not three pulses in a single trial; question marks indicate unknown latency. (C) Session sequence, shown schematically rather than to scale. Priming/habituation includes four CS-like presentations, including left-side illumination, and 15 separate short US pulses. Pre-training includes 10 CS presentations and three separate short US pulses; training includes 50 CS presentations, 46 paired long US pulses and four CS-only catch trials; testing includes 30 CS presentations and 14 separate short US pulses. Short and long US pulses last 50 and 100 ms, respectively. Interspersed short US pulses are not conditioning pairings. In controls, long-US times follow the paired schedule while training CS onsets are randomized per fish.

(D) Distal cumulative tail-angle traces from an example Delay fish (20221115_07), centred on each trial’s median over [−15, 0) s before CS onset. (E) Raw vigor (rad/ms) and scaled-vigor strips for the same five trials (9, 17, 63, 66 and 93), using the stored vigor and measured CS-relative times in D’s frame table. Both panels use identical frame selections, event guides and row positions; block names appear above trial numbers. Trial 9 is pre-training. For this exploratory draft, the existing E detected-bout eligibility mask is joined by trial and frame ID; unmatched or ineligible frames are excluded. Positive finite raw vigor is log-transformed and processed with the half-second bin, baseline and scaling recipe described below. All 80 bins from −20 to 20 s are displayed. The raw display is limited to 1 rad/ms (197 eligible frame values exceed the limit); this visual cap does not alter numerical values. CS onset and offset are green guides (offset dashed), and actual US onset is purple. E is recomputed for the D input and is not the exact frozen V12 row; the accepted E and F–H sources are preserved.

(F–H) Example heatmaps for Delay (20221115_07), 3 s Trace (20230310_08) and Control (20221115_09). Vigor is calculated from distal angular speed using the specified movement detector. Finite, strictly positive, valid adjacent detected-bout frames are log-transformed; other frames are excluded before taking the median within each left-inclusive, right-exclusive 0.5 s bin. The baseline is the median of finite bin medians over [−15, 0) s, with one vote per bin. For bin deviation d from that baseline, the displayed value is 0.7d / max(−P10, P90), where the percentiles are computed from baseline-bin deviations using linear quantiles. At least 10 finite baseline bins and a noncollapsed baseline spread are required. Black cells indicate missing values, not zero activity. Colours saturate at ±1 without clipping the numerical values; 15 draft-E bins exceed the colour limits. Timing in F–H uses the established cadence reconstruction; no additional analytical smoothing is applied. Arrowheads in F mark the five Delay-fish trials shown in D/E (9, 17, 63, 66 and 93); horizontal boundaries separate session phases. These panels are descriptive examples and do not support population-level inference.
