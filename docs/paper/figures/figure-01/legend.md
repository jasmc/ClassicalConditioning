Figure 1 | Experimental timing and example behavioural responses.

(a) Preparation and illumination. CS, conditioned stimulus; US, unconditioned stimulus. (b) Control, Delay, 3sTrace and 10sTrace trial timing. CS duration is 10 s; paired US onsets are 9, 13 and 20 s. Control dots illustrate alternative US onsets across trials; question marks indicate unknown latency. (c) Session sequence (schematic): priming/habituation, pre-training, training and testing. Short and long US pulses last 50 and 100 ms. Separate short US pulses are not conditioning pairings.

(d, e) Five trials from Delay fish 20221115_07. Tail-angle traces (d) are centred on the pre-CS median. Black traces (e) show raw-frame baseline-centred vigor, scaled using baseline deviation quantiles without logarithms, smoothing or resampling. The 0–5 display range clips values outside it. Colour bands copy the exact robust-normalized 1-s heatmap rows in g; their normalization differs from the black traces. The shared colour bar applies to these bands and to the heatmaps in f–h. Green guides mark CS onset and offset (dashed); purple guides mark measured US onset.

(f–h) Example heatmaps: Control (20221115_09), Delay (20221115_07) and 3sTrace (20230419_10). Eligible positive detected-bout vigor is natural-log transformed; direct 1-s bin medians are centred on the median baseline-bin value over [−15, 0) s. Within each fish/trial, divide the centred bins by MAD / 0.6744897501960817, where MAD is the median absolute deviation of finite baseline-bin values over [−15, 0) s. Baseline median is zero and normal-calibrated MAD is one. Robust z-scores are dimensionless; colours saturate at ±2 without numerical clipping. Missing values and rows with undefined/nonpositive baseline spread are black. Violet dotted guides show expected training US; arrowheads in g identify the d/e trials. Boundaries separate Pre, Train and Test.

Displayed trials exclude acquisition trials 1–4; selected trials are 5, 13, 59, 62 and 89. Detailed timing, eligibility, normalization and clipping definitions are retained in the figure-specific Methods record. These descriptive examples do not establish population effects.

The E colour bands and F–H heatmaps implement the author-approved 31 October robust-normalization decision as an unfrozen scientific draft; previous scoped freezes remain preserved.
