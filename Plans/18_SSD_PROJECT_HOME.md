## Cutover and retirement

1. Select one bounded batch and document its readers, writers, capacity requirements, holds and rollback location. Test a missing drive and a wrong hash. Check the genuine mounted volume before any write.
2. Stage and verify all bytes, or rehearse an explicitly reversible same-volume rename with separately verified recovery. Keep one authoritative writer. Switch readers only after old references resolve exactly and incompatible direct readers have a tested migration. Avoid broad path substitutions in scientific records.
3. Reopen the batch through its normal entry points on both systems. Recheck selection, evidence hashes, writable destinations and offline behavior. On failure, stop writes, restore the previous paths and routing, and replay those checks.
4. Record the batch receipt in Maintenance. Review any redundant copy separately with an exact list, retention reasons, verified recovery and author disposition. Candidate scratch dependencies require a recreation test before pruning.
5. If compatibility costs exceed the benefit for a role, record a decision to retain its physical root behind the common landing page. Close this plan when every role has a verified cutover or a recorded retention decision and outstanding work has a maintained owner. Remove its active navigation and preserve the committed account.
