## Individual acquisition representation

### Compare simple contrasts and corrected BLUPs first

1. Preserve the direct epoch median contrast as the transparent descriptive reference. Include an unpooled mean contrast where needed to compare like targets with a mean model. The current windows are a starting comparator, not proof that final scientific windows are approved.
2. Complete the existing corrected epoch BLUP candidate: jointly fitted condition-by-epoch fixed effects, correlated fish-specific epoch effects, and each fish's fixed-plus-random acquisition contrast.
3. Report individual acquisition separately from excess acquisition relative to the control fixed change. Include joint covariance with that shared reference; a random deviation alone is not the fish's full acquisition estimate.
4. Evaluate condition-specific residual variance and justified within-epoch serial dependence using scheduled gaps. Propagate fixed-effect, variance/correlation and individual prediction uncertainty through the complete refitted procedure.
5. Separate acquisition magnitude, maintenance and later recovery. Do not require recovery to count acquisition expression. A nonsignificant maintenance slope does not prove stability.
6. Retain every fish in the inventory, with contributing trials, technically missing windows, no-bout windows, estimability, precision and reasons. Mathematical estimability or stronger pooling does not establish sufficient individual information.

### Add individual trajectories only if they add reliable information

Compare a hierarchical individual GAMM if acquisition shape, maintenance or timing adds useful information beyond epoch contrasts. Assess partial pooling and whether sparse fish are being described mostly by their condition mean. State-space/change-point routes remain secondary candidates for a specific unanswered temporal question; they do not bypass existing uncertainty failures.

Validate candidate individual uncertainty using independent simulated datasets matching the real schedule, behavioral silence, tracking gaps, serial dependence and plausible gradual/abrupt effects. Assess control calibration, score stability, prediction, failures and uncertainty in model parameters. Population GAMM significance is not individual learning evidence.

Use an honestly identified existing-data validation design. Previously inspected Test observations cannot retrospectively become a prospective untouched holdout. Separate descriptive, retrospective cross-validation and genuinely independent claims; exclude evaluation outcomes from all relevant construction steps.

### Representation and figure choice

Compare continuous magnitudes, calibrated individual estimates and trajectories before considering categories. An operational learner flag requires a justified rule, meaningful-effect criterion if used, adequate individual precision and control calibration. Insufficient evidence is not proof of nonlearning, and a control-tail score or BLUP is not a biological probability of learning.

Keep one selected analysis path in main Figure 3: method definition, fish-level evidence, and a few deliberately illustrative examples of that same method. Show all fish and method disagreements in supplementary/exploratory material. Aggregate learner/nonlearner behavioral profiles remain Figure 4.
