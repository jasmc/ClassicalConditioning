# Figure records

[Current figures](../../current-figures.md) is the single readable guide to accepted panel versions and their scientific definitions. It links the proposed remainder separately.

- [Freeze index](freezes/README.md): exact accepted scopes, including superseded accepted versions preserved as history.
- [Current panel review](../../panel-review.md) and [paper review](../../paper-review.md): review questions.
- [Figure history](history/README.md): older recipes, design comparisons and source narratives.
- [Technical references](../reference/README.md): supported rendering and assembly tools.

The versioned [element/style specification](FIGURE_ELEMENT_SPECIFICATION.md) retains its exact bytes and governs an explicitly requested new freeze. Its linked LME critique and SciFigEditor source review retain their old paths for compatibility; they are source/history evidence, not alternate current definitions. Two short compatibility pages keep its older guide links working.
