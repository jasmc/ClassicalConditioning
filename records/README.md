# Preserved records

Documentation explains current work; records preserve the evidence and exact versions behind it.

- [Frozen analyses](frozen-analyses/README.md): immutable accepted definitions, manifests, settings and exact code; [scope index](frozen-analyses/selections.md).
- [Scientific evidence](scientific-evidence/README.md): audits, decisions, legacy descriptions and design records.
- [Figure history](figure-history/README.md): previous recipes and development narratives.
- [Figure reviews](figure-reviews/README.md): version-bound measurement and review evidence; the scoped presentation pointer lives with its figure in docs.
- [Repository archive](repository-archive/README.md): transfer manifests, hashes, recovery indexes and path aliases.
- [Repository history](repository-history/README.md): completed maintenance and handoffs.

Permanent figures, reviews and numerical payloads remain on JOAQUIM. Published checkpoints and frozen evidence retain their bytes; navigation and recovery indexes can evolve without replacing historical identities. Use [paper figures](../docs/paper/figures/README.md) for current selections and work.
